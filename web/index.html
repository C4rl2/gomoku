Rules 01

Welcome to Gomoku

Gomoku is a five-in-a-row strategy game played on a 19x19 board.

  • Blue always plays first.
  • Players place one stone on an empty intersection each turn.
  • A line of five stones can win, unless it can still be broken by capture.
  • Capturing five pairs also wins the game.
Rules 02

Captures

You capture a pair by flanking two adjacent opponent stones with your own stones.

Each capture removes two stones. Capture five pairs, ten stones total, and the game ends immediately.

Pink 4 caps
Rules 03

Free-three

A free-three is a three-stone alignment that can become an open four if it is not blocked right away.

Rules 04

Double-three rule

A move is forbidden when it creates two free-threes at the same time. A double-three created by capturing is still legal.

Rules 05

Endgame captures

A five-stone line wins only if the opponent cannot break it by capturing a pair.

If that capture would be the fifth captured pair, the opponent wins by capture instead.
